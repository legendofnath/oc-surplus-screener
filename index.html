How scoring works (every weight is an ASSUMPTION)
  • Zoning /25: 25 if on a SCAG housing-element site or residential zoning; 15 for commercial, office, retail, hotel or Specific Plan (ASSUMPTION: a specific plan may or may not allow housing); 5 for public, industrial or open space; 0 if unknown.
  • Size /20: full marks at 2 acres or more, scaled below that; 0 if acreage is unknown.
  • Existing use /15: vacant 15, parking 12, gas station 8, nothing listed 5, golf, open space or trail 2, anything else 3.
  • Hazards /15: start at 15, minus 8 in a FEMA special flood hazard area, minus 7, 5 or 2 for CAL FIRE Very High, High or Moderate. A hazard that could not be checked is listed as missing, never treated as safe.
  • Transit /15: 15 if inside a Caltrans high-quality transit area.
  • Recency /10: 10 for a 2026 notice, minus 2 for each year older, never below 0.

These are our own weights, not a legal rule. This is a screening aid, not an eligibility determination.
Data: HCD Surplus Land Act notices (data.ca.gov), SCAG zoning and housing-element sites, FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer, CAL FIRE hazard severity zones (older vintage), Caltrans high-quality transit areas.
